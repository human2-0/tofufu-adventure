# Tofufu Castle surfaces

Three original opaque albedo images created with built-in imagegen on 2026-10-07.
The exact prompts and source paths are preserved in `generation.json`.

- `basalt-masonry.png`: chipped volcanic ashlar for walls, towers and buttresses.
- `ash-flagstones.png`: worn pavers for floors, stairs, coping and stone ornaments.
- `royal-crimson-weave.png`: crimson and gold textile for banners, books and the royal carpet.

`CastleMaterials` shares materials and projects them in world space, maintaining
stone scale on long walls, slopes and shortened maze cutaways. Small relief is
derived in the surface shader. Original generated pixels are preserved unchanged.

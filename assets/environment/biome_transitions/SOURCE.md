# Shared terrain grain

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-15. Final asset: ground-grain.png.
Applied with repeating world-space UVs at nine metres per tile on meadow, desert,
jungle, frost and the new mainland shoulders. Terrain collision shares the rendered
shoulder triangles; existing authored regions retain their floor ownership.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless repeating ground microdetail texture for the Tofufu Adventure storybook 2.5D game. Square top-down orthographic neutral pale warm grey ground, fine softly hand-painted mottling of sandy soil, faint tiny grains and sparse tiny rounded pebbles. Low contrast, no distinct objects, no grass blades, no directional light or cast shadows, no perspective, no text, no grid. Seamlessly tileable on all edges. Neutral desaturated colours suitable for multiplication over green grass, golden sand and pale snow so it unifies biome materials without changing their authored palette. Entire image is the surface.

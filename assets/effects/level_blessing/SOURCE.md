# Level blessing

`halo.png` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-16 for Tofufu Adventure.

Prompt: One fantasy blessing VFX sprite for the cozy chibi tofu game; a circular golden-mint magical seal seen straight from above, pale warm gold ring, small leaf motifs, cream sparkles, hollow transparent center, hand-painted style, transparent padding, no perspective, shadows, text or watermark.

The original generated RGBA output is retained unchanged. `LevelBlessing` applies it to three rising transparent Sprite3D rings and twelve small orbiting motes. The effect follows the actor and deletes itself after 3.2 seconds. Bengal hand animations reuse the existing generated paw and claw textures without changing those assets.

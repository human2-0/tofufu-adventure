# Textured manga macaw

`feather.svg` is an original vector texture authored for this project. Its shaft,
barbs and vane shading are mapped onto closed feather meshes. Mipmaps and
anisotropic filtering keep the feather markings stable as the bird moves. The runtime body,
face, saddle, layered wings and tail are authored in `ParrotPlumage`; the four
surfaces and texture/material are shared across perched, ridden and parked birds.
No external model or texture was downloaded. Full source detail is preserved.
